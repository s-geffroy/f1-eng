## What he did

John Crosthwaite designed Mickey Thompson's [rear-engined]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/glossary/#moteur-central-arriere) car, with a production Buick V8, which Dan Gurney drove in the 1962 Indianapolis 500. It was the only rear-engined car in the field, a few years after the revolution that [John Cooper]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/john-cooper.md %}) had imposed in F1.

## What is known of his career

The rest of his career rests solely on the recollections of Barrie Wills, a former Reliant executive, published on a blog: spells at Cooper, at Team Lotus and at Dolphin in the United States; a return to Britain at the request of [Tony Rudd]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/tony-rudd.md %}) to design the BRM F1 car; then, from 1966, a role as chassis consultant at Reliant. The 1950s Lotus photos, including the one of [Mike Costin]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/mike-costin.md %}) on this site, were uploaded to Wikimedia Commons by his family.

## A short entry

For lack of first-rank sources on his F1 career, this site sticks to Indianapolis 1962 and flags the rest as testimony.
