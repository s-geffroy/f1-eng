## What he did

John Crosthwaite was largely responsible for designing Mickey Thompson's [rear-engined]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/glossary/#moteur-central-arriere) car, with a production Buick V8, which Dan Gurney drove in the 1962 Indianapolis 500. It was the only rear-engined car in the field, a few years after the revolution that [John Cooper]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/john-cooper.md %}) had imposed in F1.

## Indianapolis 1962

- **The project.** According to Motor Sport, Thompson funded his bid through the Harvey Aluminum company and obtained GM's new aluminium-block V8. The car was "designed largely" by Crosthwaite, an ex-pat Englishman; Gurney's was the only one of Thompson's cars to qualify.
- **The race.** According to the Indianapolis Motor Speedway's official results, the car, entered as the "Thompson Harvey Aluminum" with a Thompson chassis and a Buick engine, started eighth and retired on lap 92 with a rear-end failure: Gurney was classified twentieth. Motor Sport writes that it lasted until half-distance before the diff broke. Gurney defends it: "I will stand up for that 1962 car."

## What is known of his career

The rest of his career rests mainly on the recollections of Barrie Wills, a former Reliant executive, published on a blog: spells at Cooper, at Team Lotus and at Dolphin in the United States; a return to Britain at the request of [Tony Rudd]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/tony-rudd.md %}) to design the BRM F1 car; then, from 1966, a role as chassis consultant at Reliant. Only his work for Reliant is corroborated by a first-rank source: in June 1970, Bill Boddy credited Crosthwaite in Motor Sport with the rigid, braced chassis of the Reliant Scimitar GTE. The 1950s Lotus photos, including the one of [Mike Costin]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/mike-costin.md %}) on this site, were uploaded to Wikimedia Commons by his family.

## A short entry

For lack of first-rank sources on his F1 career, this site sticks to Indianapolis 1962 and the Reliant, and flags the rest as testimony.
