## What he did

Ettore Zaccone Mina is the engine engineer of the **Lancia D50**. With [Vittorio Jano]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/vittorio-jano.md %}), he opted for an engine as small, compact and light as possible, since the power of a naturally aspirated 2.5-litre would never be spectacular.

- The D50's V8 was drawn by Zaccone Mina under the supervision of Jano, who was responsible for the whole car. It is a 90° V8, producing around 250 bhp at 7,700 rpm, which made its debut at the end of 1954.
- Having passed to Ferrari along with the Lancia equipment, this engine was developed further and contributed to Juan Manuel Fangio's title in 1956.

## Attribution of the D50

Zaccone Mina disputes that Jano was the car's sole designer: in his view, Jano was the team leader, not the draughtsman, and no one is solely responsible for the whole. The angled [stressed engine]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/glossary/#moteur-porteur), the fuel tanks between the wheels: "these things all came out of discussions between us", with Gianni Lancia taking the final decisions.
