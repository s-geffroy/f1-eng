## What he did

Steve Wise is the engineer behind the **electronic hardware** of Williams [active suspension]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/glossary/#suspension-active). In late 1987, he joined Williams on the same day as [Paddy Lowe]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/paddy-lowe.md %}), with the shared task of creating an electronics department.

- The roles were divided: hardware for Wise, software for Lowe. [Frank Dernie]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/frank-dernie.md %}) recalls finding "two young guys", one for the hardware, the other for the programming.
- For the 1990 test programme, he designed a new Williams control unit and a new data logger, with more advanced processors.
- [Patrick Head]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/patrick-head.md %}) names him among the engineers responsible for the various active suspension systems and their development.
- He later became head of electronics at Williams.
