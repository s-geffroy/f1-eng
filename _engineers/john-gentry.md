## What he did

John Gentry was the **design draughtsman** who worked alongside [Rory Byrne]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/rory-byrne.md %}) on the Toleman TG280 Formula 2 car. Before Toleman, he detailed cars designed by others, at March, Shadow, Fittipaldi and Tyrrell; afterwards, he was mainly a race engineer, in F1 and then in motorcycle Grands Prix.

## Before Toleman

Motor Sport ran a profile of him in 2021 (Damien Smith); unless stated otherwise, the facts below come from it, and rest on his own recollections.

- **Training.** Born in Kingston upon Thames in 1950, he left school at 15 without qualifications; he learned his trade as a draughtsman in a contract drawing office, then at AC Cars.
- **March, 1970.** Hired as a draughtsman by [Robin Herd]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/robin-herd.md %}) and Geoff Ferris; he made proper drawings of the parts the mechanics had been making from their own sketches.
- **Shadow.** With Andy Smallman, he produced all the detail drawings for the F1 and Can-Am cars designed by [Tony Southgate]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/tony-southgate.md %}): Southgate set the overall car and the areas he wanted to dictate, and the rest was up to them.
- **Fittipaldi, 1976.** He worked on the FD04 and began to do race engineering.
- **Tyrrell, 1977.** [Maurice Philippe]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/maurice-philippe.md %}) called him in to help with the detail work on the Tyrrell 008. While [Derek Gardner]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/derek-gardner.md %}) was still there, the two men drew in a double garage, outside the drawing office. The 008 was to hide its radiators under the car and cool them with a [fan]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/glossary/#voiture-a-ventilateur) driven off the crankshaft; the team made the fan vanes itself, but the system got too hot and the radiators were moved on top of the bodywork. Autosport (April 2026) gives the same account.
- **ATS, 1978.** Herd brought him into ATS, where he engineered Jean-Pierre Jarier and Jochen Mass. According to OldRacingCars, he developed the ATS HS1s and designed the ATS D1, a "wing car", but left the team after the German Grand Prix, before it was complete. Gentry recalls that when he left, ATS owner Günther Schmid sent the police to his house, accusing him of taking the drawings for the new car.
- **March, 1979.** Teo Fabi's engineer in Formula 2, he also designed a Group 5 BMW M1 commissioned from March.

## At Toleman

- According to Motor Sport in 1980, he joined Toleman in August 1979; he was best known for his work at Shadow.
- **The TG280.** "Rory did the bodywork and I did the rest of it," Gentry sums up: they were at first set up in Tom Walkinshaw Racing's small drawing office, just the two of them.
- In Formula 2, he was also Derek Warwick's race engineer, while Byrne looked after Brian Henton.
- In 1984, he was among the engineers who accompanied Ayrton Senna's F1 debut at Toleman, with Byrne and [Pat Symonds]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/pat-symonds.md %}). He was then Johnny Cecotto's engineer; after Cecotto's accident at Brands Hatch, he decided to leave. Toleman remains his longest spell with a single team, five years.

## After Toleman

- **Renault, 1985.** After a brief spell at Alfa Romeo (Euroracing), which did not pay him, he was called in by Warwick to Renault, where he was only a race engineer: the design office did not want him at a drawing board.
- **Brabham, 1986.** Elio de Angelis's engineer, he was at the Paul Ricard test where de Angelis was killed. He considered the wheelbase of [Gordon Murray]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/gordon-murray.md %})'s BT55 far too long: after Warwick crashed at Donington, Bernie Ecclestone told him to make the car work, and the team chopped eight inches off the back of the damaged tub.
- **Afterwards.** Benetton, then motorcycle Grands Prix with Suzuki, a brief return to F1 at Leyton House, motorcycles again with Kenny Roberts's Yamaha from 1992 to 1994, TWR's Volvo in the British Touring Car Championship and, in 1996, the founding of Triple Eight with Warwick.

## Attribution

Gentry is a detail draughtsman and a race engineer, not the designer of the cars he worked on: the 008 is Philippe's, the Shadows Southgate's. On the TG280, the split "bodywork for Byrne, the rest for Gentry" is his own version; Motor Sport, in 1980, presents him only as the design draughtsman alongside Byrne. Only the ATS D1 is credited to him as his own design, by OldRacingCars, a secondary source, and he did not finish it.

## Where sources disagree

- **When he joined Toleman**: August 1979 according to Motor Sport in 1980; "the end of 1979" in the 2021 profile.
- **His teams**: OldRacingCars lists only Alfa Romeo, ATS, Shadow and Toleman in F1; its ATS D1 page calls him "formerly of Shadow and Tyrrell". Motor Sport (2021) and Autosport (2026) add March and Fittipaldi.
