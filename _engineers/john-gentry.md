## What he did

John Gentry is the **design draughtsman** who worked alongside [Rory Byrne]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/rory-byrne.md %}) on the Toleman TG280 Formula 2 car.

- He joined Toleman in August 1979; he was best known for his work at Shadow.
- In Formula 2, he was also Derek Warwick's race engineer.
- In 1984, he was among the engineers who accompanied Ayrton Senna's F1 debut at Toleman, with Byrne and [Pat Symonds]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _engineers/pat-symonds.md %}).

## A short entry

Gentry only appears in articles devoted to Byrne or Toleman, and no technical contribution of his own is detailed there. This site sticks to those mentions.
